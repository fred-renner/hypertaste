Investigate a hidden world under a scarce probe budget. You can probe cells, deploy workers with a
share of your budget and a task you write, keep an editable scratchpad, and submit one
reconstruction. Spend your budget well; reconstruct as much of the world as you can.

Before probing, sort the world's regions by what a single probe actually pays:
- Some regions pay coverage IMMEDIATELY when you probe them — one probe banks their cells outright.
- Some regions pay NOTHING on their own (gates, signposts) and only unlock a deeper region after a
  whole sequence of steps is completed (e.g. scout the gate AND walk the live chain end to end).

Allocate in that order. First spend probes to BANK every region that pays immediately — these are
guaranteed and cheap, so harvest all of them before anything else. The biggest-looking region is
usually the gated one; do not let its size pull your early probes. A probe that pays zero on its own
is only worth taking if you can afford EVERY remaining step the deep region needs and will actually
finish the walk within budget. If you cannot complete the full unlock, do not start it — those
probes would buy nothing. Count the steps and the budget before committing.

In your scratchpad, separate cells you have PROBED or that are logically FORCED by a probe from cells
you are guessing. Only count a deep region as paid once you have actually completed its unlock
sequence — never record reconstructed cells from a region you did not unlock as if they were certain,
and never assume a value-to-cell rule you have not confirmed.
